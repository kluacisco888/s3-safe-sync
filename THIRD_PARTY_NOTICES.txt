S3 Vault Sync includes or depends on the following open-source software:

- @fyears/rclone-crypt (MIT), including Rclone Crypt format support.
  https://github.com/fyears/rclone-crypt
- node-diff3 (MIT), used for three-way Markdown merging.
  https://github.com/bhousel/node-diff3
- AWS Crypto and Smithy JavaScript packages (Apache-2.0), used for SigV4.
  https://github.com/aws/aws-sdk-js-v3
- fast-xml-parser (MIT), used to read Amazon S3 ListObjectsV2 responses.
  https://github.com/NaturalIntelligence/fast-xml-parser

Remotely Save behavior and its Apache-2.0 documentation/tests were consulted
for compatibility. Its PolyForm Strict pro/src synchronization implementation
was not copied.
