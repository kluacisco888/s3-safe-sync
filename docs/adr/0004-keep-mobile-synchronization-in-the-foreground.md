# Keep mobile synchronization in the foreground

The Android and iOS plugin will synchronize while Obsidian is running in the foreground. Final local materialization is journaled and recoverable after interruption, while network transfer and decryption restart on the next run. Automatic sync and every manual operation share one serialized queue. Local edits, manual actions, and final-sync requests received during an active sync are coalesced into a follow-up run, while a redundant periodic or foreground Head poll is skipped when synchronization or Head-retry backoff is already active. The plugin requests one best-effort final sync when the app becomes hidden or the page begins unloading without starting a scheduled full integrity pass, but the mobile host may still terminate before that request completes. Reliable execution after Obsidian closes would require native host capabilities or a companion application outside the scope of a pure Obsidian plugin; the next foreground startup scan remains the recovery guarantee.
